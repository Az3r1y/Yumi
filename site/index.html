What he does

You ask, he does it.

He creates a note, adds a line to it, sets a reminder, blocks time in your calendar, starts a focus session or sums up your day. Before he creates or changes anything, he shows you exactly what he is about to do and waits for your click. Then he checks it is really done.

One thing at a time.

Your music, your next meeting, today's reminders, the weather, your GitHub activity. Folded, he shows what matters now. Open, you move from one module to the next. New modules arrive with every release.

He remembers, and speaks first.

What you tell him stays in a plain text file on your Mac that you can read, fix and erase. He speaks up on his own, rarely: two hours without a break, a meeting coming up, an agent waiting for your answer.

For developers: he follows Claude Code.

Every session, in every terminal and editor, shows up in the notch: thinking, working, done or failed. When Claude Code asks for a permission, the island opens and you answer from there, without switching windows.

Trust

  • Nothing without your click. Yumi changes nothing on your Mac without your answer, and no Claude Code permission is granted without you.
  • No account. No sign-up, no server of ours.
  • No telemetry. No analytics, no tracking. Not in the app, not on this site.
  • Your data stays on your Mac. Your calendar, your reminders and his memory never leave it. Only the services you turn on are contacted, and the AI engine you choose.

Install the alpha

  1. 1

    Download the zip.

    On the release page, take Yumi-0.1.0-alpha.zip.

    Release page
  2. 2

    Drag Yumi into Applications.

    Open the zip, then drag the app into your Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open it anyway.

    This alpha is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click « Open Anyway ». Only once.

  4. 4

    He shows up in the notch.

    Right-click him for the settings. If you use Claude Code, he offers to follow your sessions: optional, and he shows you the change before writing it.

You need

  • macOS 15 or later.
  • An AI engine for the chat and the actions: Claude Code, an Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini key, or Ollama running on your Mac (free, nothing leaves your Mac).

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Yumi is free and open source. The chat goes through the AI engine you choose: your Claude Code plan, your own API key, or Ollama for free on your Mac.

What about my data?

It stays on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. His memory is a plain text file you can read, fix or erase from his settings. Only the services you turn on are contacted.

Why does macOS block the first launch?

Because this alpha is not notarized by Apple yet. You allow it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, with « Open Anyway ». Every release is built by GitHub from the public code, and you can verify it.

Does it work on a Mac without a notch?

Not tested yet. Yumi is made for the notch; Macs without one will come later.

What languages does he speak?

English and French, following your Mac's language.

And on Windows?

Not planned for now. Yumi is a native macOS app.

How do I give feedback?

In Yumi's settings, « Send feedback » opens a form already filled in with your Yumi and macOS versions, no account needed. You can also open an issue on GitHub. Every bit of feedback helps, even a small detail.
